# Finance-and-Sales-Analytics-of-Electronics-Manufacturer

## Context and Business Problem

AtliQ Hardware Manufactures Computer Hardware such as Keyboards, Mice, SSDs, routers, etc.
They manufacture the products, store them in warehouses, and sell them to companies like Amazon, Chroma, and Walmart.
They also sell products directly to consumers through their own physical and e-commerce stores.


The company faces a serious issue in making informed, data driven decisions.
Numerous Excel files are scattered across multiple departments, making it impossible 
  -  to avoid Conflicting numbers in meetings
  -  to do seamless cross-departmental analysis
  -  to implement access control
  -  to ensure easy scalability.


## Business Objectives
  - Identify potential business expansion opportunities in promising countries.
  - Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and strategic planning.
  - Manage cost, profitability, and market share goals.
  

## Operational Objectives

  - Determine exclusivity deals and seasonal promotions.
  - Assist in determining performance bonuses & market share goals.
  - Analyse key P&L metrics and trends from a single source of truth across departments.
  - Analyse market and customer trends for swift and informed decisions.

## Tech Stack

  - Power Query
  - MS Excel
  - Data Modelling

## Skills Learned

  -  ETL using Power Query (Data cleaning and Data transformation)
  -  Data Modelling
  -  Dax
  -  Pivot Table
  -  Designing user-centric reports for readability
  -  Clear understanding of Sales & Finance metrics
